Rules

Keep the brand recognizable everywhere.

The amber dotAlways keep the amber dot on the i. It's the smallest part of the logo and the most recognizable.
BackgroundsUse the white logo on Ink or photos with a dark overlay, and the black logo on white. Don't place it on busy images.
SpaceLeave clear space around the logo of at least four times the dot's width. Never stretch or recolor it.
TypeHeadlines in Poppins SemiBold, body in Poppins or Inter Regular, numbers in Geist Mono on the website.
ChartsRising amber line, Carbon area below. Use Gain green and Loss red only for real performance numbers.
HonestyMark example numbers as examples and add "Not financial advice" on posts with returns.
