# Investedia: competitors (public info)

Category: free market data + investing education websites (Investedia: "Invest with clarity": markets, trends, learning resources).
Stats are third-party estimates (Semrush/others), not official, and dated differently. Re-check in Similarweb/Semrush for the same month.

| Competitor | What they do | Est. traffic | What works for them |
|---|---|---|---|
| TradingView | charts + social network of traders | ~209M visits/mo (Semrush, Apr 2026) | community ideas, free tier, embeddable charts |
| Finviz | stock screener, heat map | ~17.8M visits/mo (Semrush, Dec 2025) | one iconic free tool (heat map/screener) |
| Stock Analysis | free stock data for regular investors, founded 2019 by one person (Kris Gunnars) | ~8.4M visits/mo (Semrush, Jan 2026) | one page per ticker/ETF ranks in Google; free newsletter (Market Bullets, ~168k readers reported); Pro plan + ads |
| Investopedia | finance dictionary + articles | not found in this search | owns definitions ("what is an ETF"); short TikTok explainers |
| Yahoo Finance | quotes, news | not checked | brand + news |

## What Investedia can do (ranked: impact ÷ effort)
1. **Pick one niche to own** (e.g. "markets explained for young Europeans" or "clarity: one chart, one sentence"). The giants are broad; Stock Analysis won with one clear promise.
2. **SEO pages that answer one question** (each ticker/term: what it is, the chart, plain-language "why it moved"). Stock Analysis' traffic comes mostly from ticker searches.
3. **Free daily/weekly email** ("3 market moves explained in 60 seconds"): builds an audience you own; proven by Market Bullets.
4. **Short-form education** (Reels/TikTok): one concept per video, strong first 2 s, "send this to someone who…" for shares. Top-of-funnel to the site.
5. **One signature free tool** (like Finviz's heat map), e.g. a "what moved today and why" page.
Compliance: educate, don't advise (see research star "Finfluencer rules").
