# Workflow: content (Investedia posts and reels)
Merges: investedia-post, compliance-check, weekly content. Skill: `content`.

| Contract | |
|---|---|
| **Inputs** | a topic or "make content"; research stars as sources; brand kit (`brand/`) |
| **Outputs** | `content/investedia/<date>/`: reels 1080×1920 MP4 with voice-over + subtitles, carousels 1080×1350, captions, sources, math, compliance table (`content/investedia/tools/render.js`, free) |
| **Steps** | one idea per piece from the pillars → primary-source facts or math labeled "illustrative, not a forecast" → script (hook in 2 s, a reason to save/send) → render → check frames → **compliance gate** (below) → save. Never publish; the owner posts |
| **Compliance gate (all must PASS)** | 1 no "buy/sell/hold X" · 2 facts sourced, opinions labeled · 3 true, fair, not misleading, risks next to returns · 4 no promotion of CFDs/forex/futures/volatile crypto (if mentioned: you can lose 100%) · 5 sponsors/affiliates/own positions disclosed · 6 clear who speaks · 7 disclaimer on asset + caption · 8 no live prices (data licence). Sources: ESMA BE factsheet Jan 2026 (stars r-be-factsheet, r-esma-social) |
| **Failure modes** | made but never posted; legal slip; broken render (font/logo) |
| **Approval point** | owner merges the Monday content PR, then posts |
| **Metric / target** | content PRs merged ≥70%; posts published ≥3/week (Instagram Insights) |
| **Kill criterion** | 3 weeks <1 post published → stop auto-drafting, make content only on request |
