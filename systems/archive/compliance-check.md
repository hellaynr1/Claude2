# System: compliance-check (finance content)

**Trigger:** "check this post", before any Investedia post, video, newsletter or page is marked ready
**Roles:** compliance reviewer (strict), editor
**Evidence:** ESMA finfluencer factsheet for Belgium (Jan 2026), EU market-abuse rules (via ESMA statement), Wikifin/FSMA guidance: see stars r-be-factsheet, r-esma-social, r-finfluencer-rules, inv-guardrails.

**Checklist (each line: PASS / FIX + suggested rewrite)**
1. **Educate, don't advise:** no "buy/sell/hold X", no personal recommendations, no "you should invest in…".
2. **Facts vs opinion:** facts have a source (named, dated); opinions are labeled as opinion.
3. **True, fair, clear, not misleading:** no guaranteed returns, no cherry-picked timeframes, examples labeled "illustrative, not a forecast"; risks mentioned next to returns.
4. **High-risk products:** no promotion of CFDs, forex, futures, risky crowdfunding or volatile crypto; if mentioned, state you can lose 100%.
5. **Disclosure:** any sponsor, gift, affiliate link or own position disclosed openly (not only a hashtag).
6. **Identity:** clear who is speaking (Investedia, education).
7. **Disclaimer:** "For education only, not financial advice." on the asset and in the caption.
8. **Data:** prices/charts only from sources we may display (see memory/projects/investedia-data-migration.md).

**Output:** verdict READY or FIX, with the exact edits. Not legal advice; when unsure, flag for a lawyer/FSMA.
