# System: research-to-brain

**Trigger:** "look up", "research", "what's the best…"
**Roles:** researcher, teacher
**Skills & tools:** web search, video-notes (for videos), web-data (for data), Second Brain

**Steps**
1. Search; prefer official and independent sources; note which sources are vendors selling something.
2. Explain the answer simply, with sources linked.
3. Save it as a **Research** star (and link it to related stars), or as a note in the Obsidian vault if it's about a project.

**Done when:** the answer is explained, sourced and saved.
