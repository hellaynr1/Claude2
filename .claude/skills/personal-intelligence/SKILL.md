# Personal intelligence

## Start of session
1. Read `memory/README.md`, the matching `memory/projects/<project>.md`, and the top entries of `brain/log.md`. Check the Drive vault only when goals or business plans matter.
2. Resume from the project's **Next step** without asking me to re-explain.

## During
- A decision with a reason → add it to `memory/DECISIONS.md` (decision · why · alternatives · reconsider when).
- A new fact or preference about me → CLAUDE.md (short). Personal details (school, job, money) → Drive vault only, never the public repo.
- Projects are connected, but never change another project because one changed: propose it.

## End of session
1. Update the project file: Status, Open, Next step (exact).
2. Log entry in `brain/log.md`; update `memory/CONTEXT.md` if the big picture changed.
3. Commit and push. Never claim something is saved, connected or tested unless it was verified.
